# The demo's data

Four recorded clips, each with its setup, its rules and the alerts Streamatic's rules raised on it, so the demo runs from a
clone. `demo.db` holds the rules and alerts, `clips/` the videos, alert pictures and the detector's tracks (`detections.json`), `cameras/` the
setup on one frame of each.

The clips and pictures are from the MEVA dataset by Kitware Inc. and IARPA, licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://mevadata.org/resources/MEVA-data-license.txt). They have been resized and re-encoded. Kitware and IARPA do
not endorse this work.
